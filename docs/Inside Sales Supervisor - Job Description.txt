Job Title:			Inside Sales Supervisor



Job Summary

The Inside Sales Supervisor will be responsible for forming and training a new inside sales team, ensuring high lead generation rates, managing commission structures, and achieving set sales targets. The success of this position will be measured through team formation, lead generation consistency, training effectiveness, commission management, performance monitoring, and sales revenue.

Job Description: 

Team Formation and Development

Work together with HR to form the inside sales team from the Philippines, Malaysia, or Indonesia as in the Organization Chart.

Lead Generation

Ensure inside sales specialists generate a high volume of qualified leads.

 Track and report the number of qualified leads daily to ensure consistency and adherence to targets.

Training and Development

Provide comprehensive training to the inside sales team.

Implement ongoing training programs to address skill gaps and product knowledge.

Measure the effectiveness of training through improved lead generation and conversion rates.

Sales Commission Management

Receive commission based on the sales made by the inside sales team.

Ensure accurate tracking and reporting of sales performance for commission calculation.

Monitor and ensure accurate commission calculations and timely pay outs.

 Track the overall sales performance and commission distribution.

Performance Monitoring and Reporting

Regularly monitor and report the performance of the inside sales team.

Weekly performance reviews and progress reports to senior management.

Identify and address any performance issues or areas for improvement promptly.

Sales Targets and Revenue Generation

Achieve set sales targets and contribute to overall revenue generation.

Set specific sales targets for each inside sales specialist.

Ensure the team meets or exceeds the collective sales target for the 3-month period.

Report on revenue generated from inside sales activities.









Job Qualifications:

Minimum 3 years of experience in a sales supervisory or management role, preferably in the retail or B2B sales environment

Proven track record of successfully leading and motivating a high-performing inside sales team

Excellent communication, interpersonal and coaching skills to effectively lead and develop your team

Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities to identify and address sales challenges

Proficient in using CRM systems and sales reporting tools

Passion for sales and a customer-centric approach
