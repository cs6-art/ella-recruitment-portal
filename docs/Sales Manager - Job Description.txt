Job Summary

The Sales Manager is responsible for developing and executing strategic sales initiatives to drive business growth in Malaysia. This role involves identifying new business opportunities, building strong customer relationships, and expanding the company's market presence. The Sales Manager will work closely with the marketing team to implement targeted campaigns, analyze market trends, and develop competitive strategies. Additionally, the role includes mentoring and leading the sales team to achieve and exceed sales targets, ensuring customer satisfaction, and driving overall revenue growth.

Job Responsibilities:

Developing and implementing effective sales strategies to expand the company's customer base and increase revenue

Identifying and pursuing new business opportunities in the Malaysian market

Building and maintaining strong relationships with both existing and prospective customers

Providing excellent customer service and support to ensure customer satisfaction

Collaborating with the marketing team to create and execute targeted marketing campaigns

Analyzing market trends and competitor activity to inform sales and business development efforts

Achieving and exceeding individual and team sales targets

Mentoring and developing the sales team to drive performance



Job Qualifications:

Proven track record of success in a sales or business development role, preferably within the copier or office equipment industry

Excellent communication, negotiation and interpersonal skills

Strong commercial acumen and the ability to understand and meet customer needs

Proficient in Microsoft Office suite and familiarity with CRM systems

Ability to work independently and as part of a team

Resilience and a results-driven mindset

Fluency in English and Mandarin
