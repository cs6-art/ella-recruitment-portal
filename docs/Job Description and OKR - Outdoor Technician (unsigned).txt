Position:		Outdoor Technician

Subject:		JOB DESCRIPTION





I understand that the company reserves the right to include, change and modify any of the job description and tasks contained or described here as it deems appropriate at any time, with or without notice.

Job Description Summary

Install and set up copiers and multifunctional devices at customer locations.

Diagnose and troubleshoot hardware and software issues with copiers and related equipment.

Perform preventive maintenance and routine servicing of copiers to ensure optimal performance.

Replace parts, components, and consumables as needed to repair copiers and restore functionality.

Provide technical support and assistance to customers via phone, email, or in-person.

Document all service activities, including repairs, replacements, and maintenance tasks.

Collaborate with sales and customer service teams to ensure customer satisfaction.

Stay updated on new copier models, technologies, and industry trends.
