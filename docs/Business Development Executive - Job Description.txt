

Job Title:			Business Development Executive



Job Summary:

The Business Development Executive is responsible for identifying and developing new business opportunities, building strong client relationships, and driving revenue growth. This role involves lead generation, sales presentations, and maintaining customer satisfaction while ensuring sales targets and key performance indicators (KPIs) are met.



Job Description:

Conduct daily cold calling, emailing and messaging to new target markets and make onsite appointments to meet prospects.

Generating leads, qualifying prospects, preparing sales quotations & business proposals and closing sales

Understand prospect needs and requirements to demonstrate the company’s products and services.

 Conduct product demonstrations and presentations to prospective clients.

Responsible for your sales performance and to meet or exceed the agreed sales targets and OKRs.

Adhere to and responsible for the outdoor pre-sales, on-sales and post-sales processes.

Learn and have an in-depth understanding and knowledge of all department processes.

Build and maintain accurate databases and records of all sales activities and customer interactions using the company’s SAP CRM.

Provide ongoing after-sales customer support to ensure customer satisfaction, build relationships and promote upselling and cross selling opportunities.

Stay informed about market trends, competition and market.

Continuously seek opportunities for personal and professional growth through training and development initiatives.

Always a Team player and work together to support self, company and colleagues.

Any other official tasks and jobs assigned by the immediate superior.



















Job Qualifications:

1–3 years of outdoor sales experience in IT, office equipment, or document management.

Strong communication, negotiation, and closing skills with a proven track record of exceeding sales targets.

Proactive, self-motivated, and results-driven with strong business acumen.

Proficient in CRM and sales enablement tools.

Ability to identify customer needs and provide tailored solutions.

Excellent networking, written, and verbal communication skills.

Professional, well-groomed, and confident.

Resourceful, adaptable, and thrives in a fast-paced environment.

Experience in the copier industry is a plus.
