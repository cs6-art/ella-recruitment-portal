

Job Title:			SAP Business One Functional Consultant

Department:			ERP

1st Reporting Office:	Technical & Support Supervisor

2nd Reporting Officer:	CEO



Job Responsibilities:	

Implement and configure SAP Business One software based on client requirements

Familiar with the SAP B1 ASAP methodology and all the SAP B1 modules

Analyze and document business processes to identify areas for improvement

Provide training and support to end users on SAP Business One functionalities

Collaborate with technical teams to troubleshoot and resolve issues

Stay updated on SAP Business One updates and enhancements

Gather business requirements from clients and design SAP B1 solutions that meet their needs.

Configure and customize SAP B1 software to meet client requirements.

Conduct system testing and work with clients to ensure successful implementation.

Provide end-user training and support to clients.

Collaborate with technical consultants to ensure seamless integration between SAP B1 and other systems.

Develop and maintain project plans, timelines, and deliverables.

Provide thought leadership and guidance to clients on best practices for utilizing SAP B1 software.



Job Qualifications:

Bachelors Degree in IT or any related field.

Minimum 3 years’ Experience in SAP B1 Implementation with SQL & HANA configuration

Familiarized with the SQL Crystal Report, Query Report, SQL Store Procedure, FMS, SQL View & Blue Print Generating











Skill Requirements:



ERP/SAP B1 Implementation/Support experience 

Dashboard Enablement 

SAP B1 SQL / SAP B1 Hana 

SQL Queries
